Architectural & Evaluator Comparison Matrix

Dimension System A: Swiss Clean (Space Grotesk) System B: Mobbin Design Language (Inter/Saans)
Design Ancestry Josef Müller-Brockmann, CERN, Dieter Rams. Mathematical asymmetric grids, bold black structural dividing rules, strict functional typography. Mobbin, Apple Human Interface, Stripe. Gallery-white canvas that gets out of the way, stadium-pill interaction, 24px card geometry.
Typography Stack Space Grotesk (700 Display) + JetBrains Mono. High-tech geometric grotesk with monospaced telemetry feeds. Bold, stark, and architectural. Inter / Saans (650 Heading + 300 Light Subtitle). Dramatic weight contrast without ornament. Tight line-height (1.0–1.13) with sentence-case periods.
Geometry & Elevation 0px Border-Radius, 2px Black Structural Hairlines. Flat, unapologetically industrial. Section numbering (`[01 // PILLARS]`) for logical ordering. 24px Card Radius, Stadium Pills (9999px), 30% Squircles. Elevation via neutral tint ladder (`#f3f3f3`, `#f0f0f0`) instead of drop shadows.
Color Discipline Stark monochrome with surgical Swiss Vermilion (#E11D48) or Cobalt (#0284C7) status markers for live Antarctic stations. Gallery-white with Electric Blue (#0066FF) strictly reserved for key decision badges. Content (photography & plots) supplies all saturation.
SIH Evaluator Impact Signals CERN-Level Research Infrastructure. Perfect for senior glaciologists and MoES directors who demand dense tabular NetCDF data and engineering rigor. Signals Tier-1 Silicon Valley Product Polish. Makes the portal look like an impeccably funded, world-class consumer-facing science platform.
