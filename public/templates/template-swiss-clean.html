41ST IAE // LARSEMANN

41st Indian Scientific Expedition to Antarctica

Deployment aboard MV Vasiliy Golovnin. Ingested 8,760 hours of continuous automated weather telemetry at Bharati Station, documenting winter blizzard wind gusts up to 134 km/h and an inland traverse toward Amery ice shelf.

Vessel: MV Vasiliy Golovnin
Lead PI: Dr. Rahul Sharma
Station: Bharati (69°S) & Maitri
Datasets: 12 NetCDF Ingests
2016
THIRD POLE // HIMANSH

Establishment of Himansh High-Altitude Cryosphere Station

Inaugurated at 4,080m elevation in the Chandra Basin (Spiti Valley, Himachal Pradesh). Serves as the national baseline monitoring site for Himalayan glacier mass balance, proglacial discharge, and supraglacial lake formation.

Elevation: 4,080 meters ASL
Glaciers: Chhota Shigri / Samudra Tapu
Lead PI: Dr. Parmanand Sharma
Monitoring: Ablation Stakes / Sonic Depth
2014
ARCTIC // INDARC MOORING

Deployment of India’s IndARC Underwater Arctic Observatory

Moored at 192 meters depth in Kongsfjorden fjord, Svalbard. Designed to survive seasonal sea ice and drifting icebergs while continuously recording water temperature, salinity, and current velocity in the high Arctic.

Location: Kongsfjorden (78°59'N)
Water Depth: 192 meters
Lead PI: Dr. K. P. Krishnan
Sensors: Seabird SBE-37 CTD / RDI ADCP
2012
31ST IAE // BHARATI

Commissioning of Bharati Scientific Research Base

Constructed from 134 modular prefabricated ISO containers on elevated steel stilts in the Larsemann Hills. Features aerodynamic geometry preventing snow drift buildup and 42% thermal cogeneration.

Coordinates: 69°24'S, 76°11'E
Architectural Grid: 134 ISO Containers
Thermal Recovery: 42% Cogeneration
Capacity: 47 Summer / 24 Winter
1989
8TH IAE // MAITRI

Establishment of Maitri Base in the Schirmacher Oasis

India's second permanent research base established on the rocky ice-free oasis beside freshwater Lake Priyadarshini. Primary center for geomagnetism, seismology, and Dobson total column ozone tracking.

Coordinates: 70°45'S, 11°43'E
Geology: Schirmacher Rock Oasis
Freshwater Source: Lake Priyadarshini
Active Since: 1989 (Continuous)
